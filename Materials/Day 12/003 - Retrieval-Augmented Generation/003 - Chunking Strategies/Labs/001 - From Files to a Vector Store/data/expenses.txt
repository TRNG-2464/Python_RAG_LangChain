Expense Reports

Expense reports are due by the 5th of each month for the previous month.
Reports submitted after the 10th are held until the following cycle.
Reimbursement is issued within 21 days of approval.

Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for every expense over 25 dollars. A credit
card statement is not an acceptable substitute for a receipt. Expenses
submitted without a required receipt are rejected and must be resubmitted.

Travel

Air travel must be booked at least 21 days before departure through the
company travel portal. Economy class is the standard for all flights under
six hours. Hotel stays are capped at 200 dollars per night, excluding tax.
Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day.
