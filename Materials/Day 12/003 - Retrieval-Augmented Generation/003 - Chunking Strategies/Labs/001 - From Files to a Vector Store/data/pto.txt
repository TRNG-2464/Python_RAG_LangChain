Paid Time Off

Employees accrue 15 days of paid time off each year. Accrual begins on the
first day of employment and is credited monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per
month. Unused days may be carried into the following year up to a maximum of
10 days; anything beyond that is forfeited on December 31.

Requesting Time Off

Time off requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 14 days in
advance. Requests of more than five consecutive days require approval from a
department manager. Requests submitted with less than 14 days notice are
handled case by case and are not guaranteed.

Sick Leave

Sick leave is separate from paid time off. Employees receive 8 sick days per
year. Sick days do not carry over and are not paid out on departure. A note
from a physician is required for any absence longer than three consecutive
days.
